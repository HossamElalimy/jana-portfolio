## Intent

A launching-soon teaser for Jana Ezzeldin's portfolio site, made to market it to her
own audience. Concept chosen from five pitches: **The Rally**. One tennis ball carries
the whole film. It is served off the racket in *Figure in Motion*. Every bounce lands
on the next painting, and that painting grows out of the point of contact. The last
bounce becomes the dot in the JANA wordmark. The camera chases the ball. The rhythm is
uneven, with fast bounces next to still holds. Small elements sit on a lot of paper,
and something carries across every boundary (onetake craft, inside a HyperFrames
composition).
